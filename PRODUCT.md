# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

DogAdopt is a demo and portfolio project. Its audience includes prospective employers and dog adoption organizations evaluating the work, as well as prospective dog adopters exploring the demonstrated experience. Volunteers and fosters are secondary audiences.

## Product Purpose

Demonstrate a dog adoption website and the developer's frontend work. Visitors can explore the concept of finding a dog, learning about the organization, and starting an adoption application.

## Operating Context

The existing implementation is a static, multipage HTML, CSS, and JavaScript website. HomePage.html is its home entry. Other pages cover dog listings, About, adoption, contact, sign-in, and sign-up. about.js generates the team cards on AboutUs.html.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Local JavaScript supplies dog listings and team profiles.
- The adoption form displays a local success notification and resets the form. It does not submit an application to a service.
- Authentication and contact screens are demonstrations; no backend integration is present in this repository.
- Do not describe the current demo as an operational adoption service or imply that its forms create real applications or accounts.
- The user has not designated any product facts or assets as mandatory to preserve.
- Deployment requirements, service geography, and future backend scope remain undecided.

## Evidence on Hand

The user confirmed that the site's staff profiles, statistics, partnerships, and support claims are placeholders. Photos/ contains the existing dog and staff image assets. These assets and statements are demo material, not verified evidence of a real organization or its results.

## Product Principles

- Make the adoption concept understandable to prospective adopters.
- Let employers and adoption organizations evaluate the quality of the demonstrated frontend experience.
- Distinguish demo behavior and placeholder claims from real services and verified evidence.
- Include volunteers and fosters as secondary audiences without obscuring adoption tasks.
