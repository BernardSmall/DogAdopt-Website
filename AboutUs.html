About Us

We’re a community of volunteers and adopters helping dogs find loving forever homes.

Our Mission

To connect dogs with caring adopters through a simple, transparent process and lifelong support.

Our Story

Started by a small foster group, DogAdopt grew into a platform that helps shelters reach more families, reduce stays, and improve matches using personality profiles and post-adoption guidance.

What We Do

  • Partner with shelters & rescues
  • Offer training & care resources
  • Support fosters and volunteers
  • Host adoption events
