## Overall impression

The desktop opening is approachable and organized. Broken image sizing and mobile behavior undermine that first impression. Repairing those problems would make the portfolio substantially more credible to employers.

## What works

- The first desktop view separates the introduction, mission, story, and activities clearly.
- Team cards use semantic articles, name headings, and image alternatives.
- Named navigation and an active About underline orient visitors.

## Priority issues

1. **P1: Team portraits overflow.** about.js:33–38 creates img elements, but styles.css:798–803 styles their parent as a background image container. Portraits render at 1024×1536 inside roughly 302px cards, overlapping adjacent cards and later sections. Give images explicit containment, a media aspect ratio, display:block, and object-fit:cover. Suggested command: `$impeccable harden about.js`.
2. **P1: Mobile layout is unusably small.** At 390px, navigation and body copy measure 4.29px, the title 14.04px, and document width 1316px. About styles have no responsive breakpoint and the menu toggle stays hidden. Use minimum rem-based sizes, stacked grids, and a functioning compact navigation. Suggested command: `$impeccable adapt AboutUs.html`.
3. **P1: Closing actions go nowhere.** AboutUs.html:134–135 uses href="#" for adoption and volunteering; footer links at 147–149 also use placeholders. Route adoption to a working demo destination and explain unavailable demo actions. Suggested command: `$impeccable harden AboutUs.html`.
4. **P2: Demo claims read as real evidence.** The user confirmed all profiles and impact claims are placeholders. Identify the site and claims as demonstration content so an employer or adopter can interpret them correctly. The statistics panel at AboutUs.html:59–70 is also hidden from assistive technology despite carrying meaningful text. Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify AboutUs.html`.
5. **P2: Text contrast is weak.** White on #FF914D is about 2.2:1; muted #6B7280 on #F6F2ED is about 4.3:1. Use darker button text or a darker button background, and darken muted copy. Suggested command: `$impeccable colorize AboutUs.html`.

## Cognitive load and emotional journey

Three checklist failures indicate moderate cognitive load: single focus, hierarchy across the full page, and minimal choices. Chunking, grouping, one decision at a time, working memory, and progressive disclosure are adequate for this short page. The header has five navigation choices plus two account actions. Those account buttons receive more emphasis than adoption in the first view.

The friendly opening leads to unexplained statistics, then a team section disrupted by enormous images, and finally inactive calls to action. The ending needs a working next step more than additional decoration.

## Persona red flags

- First-time adopter: Adopt now changes only the URL hash; the next step remains unclear.
- Mobile visitor: 4.29px text and overflowing portraits make scanning and tapping impractical.
- Prospective employer: broken containment, dead links, and unlabelled demo claims obscure the quality of the work.

## Minor observations

The mobile toggle lacks aria-expanded and aria-controls, and no .is-open CSS implements its state. About lacks aria-current="page". script.js accesses the absent homepage dog-card container; about.js still renders because it loads separately. Decorative paws should be hidden from assistive technology. Keyboard, screen-reader, slow-network, and destination-page audits were not performed.

## Questions to consider

Would a portfolio visitor learn more from a short explanation of the implemented adoption flow than from fictional impact figures? Should reading About lead primarily to dog browsing or the application demo?
